06 / Good to know

BEFORE YOU
SHOW UP.

A few questions to make your first enquiry easier. We can update these answers with your actual gym policies later.

Can beginners join?

Many people start training as beginners. Tell the team about your experience and goals so they can explain what support is available at Unique Fitness.

How do I find current membership prices?

Send an enquiry using the form below. The team can confirm current rates, joining fees, billing frequency and membership terms.

Can I visit before choosing a plan?

Use the enquiry form to ask about membership details, available facilities and access arrangements before joining.

What should I bring for my first session?

Comfortable training clothes, suitable footwear and water are a sensible starting point. Confirm any facility-specific requirements with the gym.
