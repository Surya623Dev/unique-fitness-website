WEBSITE INFORMATION

PRIVACY NOTICE.

This notice explains how Unique Fitness handles details submitted through the website’s membership enquiry form and direct WhatsApp contact.

Information collected

The website enquiry form requests your name and phone number, and lets you optionally provide an email address and message. It also records the fitness goal and enquiry category you select. Please do not submit sensitive medical, financial or identity information.

How information is used

Enquiry details are used to respond to questions about membership options, pricing, facilities and training support. The form includes a consent checkbox for this purpose. Details should not be used for unrelated marketing without an appropriate basis and any required notice or choice.

Hosting and form storage

The website is hosted on Netlify and its enquiry form is processed through Netlify Forms. Submitted information may be stored in the site owner’s Netlify account so the enquiry can be reviewed and answered. Access should be limited to people responsible for handling enquiries, and records should be deleted when no longer needed.

WhatsApp contact

The WhatsApp button opens a chat with a pre-filled message to +91 86868 36628. It does not send a message automatically; you choose whether to send it. If you contact the gym through WhatsApp, the information you share is also handled under WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy practices.

External services

The website loads illustrative images from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may receive technical data such as your IP address and browser request when assets load.

Questions about your information

For a question or request about information you submitted to Unique Fitness, contact the gym using WhatsApp at +91 86868 36628 and mention that your request concerns website enquiry data. Requests will be handled by the site owner, subject to applicable requirements.
