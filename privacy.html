WEBSITE INFORMATION

PRIVACY NOTICE.

This page describes the intended handling of information submitted through the Unique Fitness enquiry form. The gym owner must review and confirm this notice, including contact details, retention practices and third-party services, before collecting real enquiries.

Information you may submit

The enquiry form asks for your name and phone number, and allows you to optionally provide an email address and message. It also records the training goal and enquiry type you select. Please do not submit sensitive medical or financial information.

How information is intended to be used

Information submitted through the form is intended to be used to respond to your enquiry about a visit, membership or training support. Do not use submitted information for unrelated marketing unless you have an appropriate lawful basis and provide any required notice or choice.

Website hosting and form processing

The website prototype is configured for Netlify Forms. If the site is deployed with Netlify form handling enabled, form submissions may be processed and stored by Netlify according to its terms and privacy documentation. The website owner must configure access, notifications and retention and provide the applicable details before launch. GitHub Pages does not process form submissions.

Retention, access and requests

The gym owner should define a retention period, restrict access to authorised people, protect records and document how people can request access, correction or deletion where applicable. These operational details must be confirmed before the site is used with real submissions.

External images and fonts

This prototype loads sample images from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request when assets load. Before production, the owner can replace sample imagery and choose whether to self-host fonts or use external services.

Contact

Use the contact information on the main website after it has been completed with verified details. This notice is a starter template, not legal advice. The owner should review it for the business's actual practices and applicable law before launch.
